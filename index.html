Our Mandate

Perfecting Every Man in Christ

House of Glory Christian Centre is a ministry raised in Port Harcourt, Nigeria, with a divine mandate: to present every man perfect in Christ Jesus (Colossians 1:28).

We are committed to preaching Christ, warning every man and teaching every man in all wisdom, so that no one is left behind — declaring glory through lives transformed by the Gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ.

  • ✦ Christ-centred preaching & teaching
  • ✦ A worship atmosphere charged with God's glory
  • ✦ City-wide impact from Port Harcourt
  • ✦ A family where every man is perfected
